Frequently Asked Questions

What about equipment?
Rentals are available at the pro shop, but bring your own clubs if you've got 'em. Carts are included for every foursome.
What about food arrangements?
Breakfast before the shotgun start and lunch at the turn are included. Let us know dietary needs when you register.
What about the weather?
We play rain or shine (it's the Winter Scramble, after all). Dress in layers and bring a windbreaker.
